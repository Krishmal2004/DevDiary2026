Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

DevDiary2026 is a personal work log for developers: a daily diary drafted from your GitHub activity, personal todos and email reminders. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Your GitHub profile: your GitHub user ID, username and avatar URL, received when you sign in with GitHub. If your GitHub profile email is public, it's used as your default reminder email.
  • GitHub access tokens: the user access and refresh tokens GitHub issues when you sign in. They let the app read your activity on your behalf.
  • Content you create: diary entries, todos (titles, due dates, linked URLs) and your settings (reminder email, reminder on/off, time zone).

What we read from GitHub but don't store

To draft diary entries and show the Repositories page, the app reads your repositories, commits, pull requests, reviews and issues from GitHub when you ask for them. It only sees repositories you've installed the app on, with read-only permissions. That data is shown to you and isn't saved, except for text you choose to keep in a diary entry.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in (a signed session cookie).
  • To show your diary, todos and repositories to you, and nobody else.
  • To email you when a todo you created is due, if you've turned reminders on.

We don't sell your data, show ads or use your data to train models.

Who else processes it

  • GitHub: sign-in and all repository data.
  • Our email provider (Resend or Postmark): your email address and the titles of due todos, to deliver reminders.
  • Our hosting provider: stores the database the app runs on.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you use the app. You can delete individual diary entries and todos at any time. If you revoke DevDiary2026's access in your GitHub settings (Settings → Applications → Authorized GitHub Apps), your stored GitHub tokens are deleted automatically. To delete your account and all of its data, open an issue or contact us using the link below.
